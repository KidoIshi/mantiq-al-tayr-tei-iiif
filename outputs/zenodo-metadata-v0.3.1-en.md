## Title

Manṭiq al-ṭayr in TEI and IIIF: A Two-Manuscript Transcription Prototype (BnF Persan 348 and Smithsonian FSC-MS-28)

## Description

This dataset presents a small TEI P5 and IIIF implementation for the opening of Farīd al-Dīn ʿAṭṭār’s *Manṭiq al-ṭayr*, based on two manuscript witnesses. It contains a diplomatic transcription of 30 couplets in BnF Persan 348 (dated 1492), a separate transcription of the corresponding nine opening couplets in Smithsonian FSC-MS-28 (sixteenth century), and links from 78 hemistichs to regions in three IIIF Canvases.

Automated collation of the 18 overlapping hemistichs records 16 exact matches, one textual variant (0004b), and one passage partly covered by physical repair (0006a). The variant is encoded in a non-selective TEI apparatus; neither witness is designated as the preferred reading.

The package includes a project-specific TEI customization (ODD, RELAX NG, and Schematron), IIIF AnnotationPages generated from the TEI, validation scripts and reports, and a browser-based viewer for side-by-side comparison.

This is a technical and methodological prototype, not a critical edition. The author checked the transcriptions and image regions against the manuscript images; the work has not undergone independent scholarly review. Manuscript images are not included. They are referenced through IIIF and remain subject to the terms of the Bibliothèque nationale de France and the Smithsonian Institution. Project-created data and documentation are licensed under CC BY 4.0; code is licensed under the MIT License.

AI tools were used to prepare initial transcription candidates, image-region proposals, code, and documentation drafts. The author made the editorial decisions, checked the manuscript transcriptions and image regions, reviewed the implementation structure and validation results, and approved the changes included in the release. A second AI system provided an additional code review; its findings, the resulting changes, and the subsequent checks are documented in the repository.

# v0.3.4 — Corrected IIIF regions for Persan 348 couplets 25–30

Version 0.3.4 corrects the image-region alignment of the second hemistichs in Persan 348 couplets 25–30.

- The six regions `0025b`–`0030b` were shifted horizontally from x=520–1120 to x=740–1340.
- The upper boundary of `0027b` was extended from y=870 to y=840 so that the final word `کند` is fully visible.
- The Persian transcription, collation, schemas, transformation logic, and all regions for couplets 1–24 are unchanged.
- The researcher inspected and approved all six corrected regions in the browser demonstrator.
- The IIIF AnnotationPages and validation reports were regenerated from the TEI source.

The project remains a technical and methodological prototype, not a critical edition. External manuscript images are referenced through the holding institutions’ IIIF services and are not included in the repository or archive.

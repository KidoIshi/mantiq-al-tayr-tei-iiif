# v0.3.1 — Public-metadata clarification

Version 0.3.1 clarifies the public English metadata for the two-manuscript TEI–IIIF transcription prototype. It does not change the Persian transcriptions, collation results, TEI–IIIF image regions, schemas, or validation logic released in v0.3.0.

## Changes since v0.3.0

- Replaced the potentially expansive label “multi-witness” with a direct description of the two manuscript witnesses.
- Rewrote the English title and description in factual terms and added the manuscript dates, collation counts, image-access conditions, and licence boundaries.
- Replaced the keyword “textual criticism” with terms that describe the present scope more precisely: manuscript collation, digital scholarly editing, and digital humanities.
- Clarified the division of responsibility between AI assistance and the author. AI tools prepared candidates and drafts; the author made the editorial decisions, checked the manuscript data, reviewed the implementation structure and validation results, and approved the release changes.
- Recorded the additional code review by a second AI system as supporting evidence rather than as scholarly peer review.

## Scope retained from v0.3.0

- BnF Persan 348: 30 couplets / 60 hemistichs.
- Smithsonian FSC-MS-28: the corresponding opening 9 couplets / 18 hemistichs.
- Automated collation of the 18 overlapping hemistichs: 16 exact matches, one textual variant (0004b), and one passage partly covered by physical repair (0006a).
- 39 TEI couplets, 78 hemistichs, 3 IIIF Canvases, 3 AnnotationPages, and 78 image regions checked by the author against the manuscripts.

This remains a technical and methodological prototype, not a critical edition. The work has not undergone independent scholarly peer review. External manuscript images are not redistributed and remain subject to the holding institutions’ terms.
